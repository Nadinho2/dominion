Welcome to Dominion • Est. 2002

Inspiring the Next Generation of Global Leaders

Founded in 2002 by Olwaseyi, Dominion International School was established with a singular visionary mandate: to provide an uncompromising standard of holistic education that harmonizes academic brilliance, moral fortitude, and athletic achievement.

Over more than two decades, our institution has grown into one of the premier centers of learning in Lagos. Operating from our purpose-built campus in Ajao Estate along Airport Road, Ikeja, we deliver an enriched blended British-Nigerian curriculum that prepares learners for global success while anchoring them in exemplary values.

British & Nigerian Fusion

Cambridge Primary & Checkpoint combined with national BECE & WAEC excellence.

Holistic Sports Culture

Track & field, football championships, basketball, and active house leagues.

Cutting-Edge STEM Labs

Practical science, coding, robotics, and interactive multimedia learning.

Safe & Accessible Location

24/7 security, CCTV surveillance, and school bus routes across Ikeja and environs.

Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

What curriculum does Dominion International School teach?
We operate a rich blended British-Nigerian curriculum. At the primary and lower secondary stages, students follow the Cambridge Primary and Checkpoint syllabus alongside the Nigerian national curriculum. In Senior Secondary, learners are prepared for both Cambridge IGCSE and WASSCE (WAEC) / NECO examinations.
Are school bus transportation routes available?
Yes! We maintain an air-conditioned, chaperone-supervised fleet of school buses with designated pick-up and drop-off points covering Ajao Estate, Airport Road, Ikeja GRA, Maryland, Anthony, Oshodi, Mafoluku, and Ilupeju corridors.
What age does Dominion accept into Pre-Nursery?
Our Early Years Foundation Stage admits infants and toddlers starting from 18 months into our warm, secure Pre-Nursery playgroup, equipped with dedicated nap and sensory development rooms.
How often is Inter-House Sports held?
Our flagship Inter-House Sports Festival is held annually, featuring all four school houses (Emerald, Topaz, Sapphire, and Ruby). Additionally, regular sports clubs (football, basketball, swimming, athletics, and martial arts) train weekly.
How can prospective parents book a campus tour?
You can submit your preferred date through the online tour booking form above or reach our Admissions desk directly via WhatsApp at +234 803 123 4567. We conduct guided tours Monday through Saturday.
