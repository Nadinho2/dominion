# Dominion International School Website

> **Motto:** *"Dominion Through Excellence"*  
> **Location:** Plot 14/16 Airport Road, Ajao Estate, Ikeja, Lagos State, Nigeria  
> **Founded:** 2002 by Olwaseyi

A modern, responsive institutional profile website for **Dominion International School**, showcasing our blended British-Nigerian curriculum, world-class academic activities, sports championships, campus facilities, and admissions.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

- **Blended British-Nigerian Curriculum:**
  - **Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS):** Pre-Nursery & Nursery 1–3 (Montessori-aligned).
  - **Primary School:** Basic 1–6 (Cambridge Primary Framework & Checkpoint).
  - **Junior Secondary:** JSS 1–3 (Cambridge Lower Secondary & BECE).
  - **Senior Secondary:** SSS 1–3 (Cambridge IGCSE & WASSCE / NECO).
- **Outstanding Academic Activities:**
  - STEM & Robotics Exposition
  - State-of-the-Art Science Practical Laboratories
  - Cowbellpedia & Mathematics Olympiad Training
  - Press, Debate & Model United Nations Society
  - Annual Speech & Prize-Giving Day
- **Sports Events & Inter-House Championship:**
  - Active 4-House League (Emerald, Topaz, Sapphire, Ruby) with live standings and medal counters.
  - Track & Field, Football League, Basketball, Swimming Gala, Table Tennis, and Taekwondo.
- **Tuition & Activities Fee Schedule:**
  - Transparent termly breakdown for Early Years, Primary, JSS, and SSS.
  - Optional services: Air-conditioned school bus routes (Zones A, B, C), hot lunch program, and after-school clubs.
  - **Live Interactive Fee Estimator:** Real-time calculation of termly investment.
- **Admissions & Campus Tour Booking:**
  - Step-by-step admissions walkthrough with an interactive booking form.
- **Mobile-First & Cross-Browser Responsive:**
  - Touch-swipeable tab navigation, custom vector crest, glassmorphism UI, and WhatsApp quick chat widget.

---

## 🚀 Running Locally

1. Ensure [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) is installed.
2. In the project directory, run:
   ```bash
   node server.js
   ```
3. Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```text
   http://localhost:5000
   ```
